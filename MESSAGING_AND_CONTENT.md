# MESSAGING & CONTENT STRATEGY — hero / villain, standing themes, news agent

This governs every marketing asset and every agent. It sits on top of the messaging source of
truth (the model facts and legal grounding). No em-dashes anywhere customer-facing. Never say
"gray area."

## THE CONTENT RULE (hero / villain)

- HERO: the physician and the injured worker. Our program is the tool that helps them.
- VILLAINS: the middlemen and the friction. PBMs, TPAs (claims administrators), and the
  denial / delay / utilization-review machinery that keeps injured workers from their medication
  and buries practices in administrative work.
- NEVER negative about: physician dispensing, mail-order pharmacy, or workers'-comp pharmacy.
  Two of those are our own model and some prospects dispense today. Do not disparage them,
  ever, even implicitly (no "dispensing trades one problem for another," no "operational
  burden" swipes).
- The problem we point at is the access gap and billing friction in the WC system, framed as a
  patient problem we solve, not as any pharmacy model being bad.

## ACCURACY GUARDRAIL

Our model still bills the payer through MDRx. Do NOT frame it as cutting the payer out of
paying. Aim the fire at the middlemen and the denial games (PBMs, TPAs, UR friction), where the
story is both true and infuriating to a doctor. Keep every claim inside the source of truth:
independent pharmacy, claims-purchasing / factoring (not ownership, not dispensing), WC only,
legal opinions per state, cite 700 Pharmacy and Gosfield.

## STANDING THEMES (what doctors care about, all positive-to-us framing)

1. The middlemen are getting caught. The FTC's 2026 settlements against the largest PBMs
   (Express Scripts in February, Caremark in July) are federal validation that the drug
   middlemen are a problem. Angle: route your work-comp scripts around that friction entirely.
2. Denials and delays are worsening. TPAs and utilization-review are blocking or slowing
   treatment (e.g., press calling Sedgwick "a menace to workers," 2026 UR overhauls). Angle:
   your patients get their medication anyway, next-day and at no cost.
3. Reimbursement is squeezing private practice in 2026. Angle: a compliant new revenue line
   from the claims you already generate. (Opportunity framing, never doom.)
4. Access, backed by data. WCRI / drug-trend data showing injured workers getting (or missing)
   medication. Angle: proof the access problem is real and that our route fixes it.
5. The legal footing. 700 Pharmacy and Gosfield. Straight win, always usable.

## STANDING SOURCES (the wells to keep drawing from)

- WC + pharmacy: WorkersCompensation.com (monthly WC pharmacy update), WCRI, Risk & Insurance,
  MyMatrixx Statehouse Watch, Enlyte drug trends, daisyBill (payer/TPA behavior).
- PBM / middleman: FTC newsroom (PBM actions), health-law firm alerts.
- Practice economics: Medical Economics, MGMA, Becker's Orthopedic/Spine, Physicians Practice.
- Legal: Alice Gosfield's newsletter, Dan Siegel's firm, American Health Lawyers Association.
- Primary: PA Bureau of Workers' Comp (fee schedule / formulary), DOL OWCP releases, new rulings.

## NEWS-MONITORING AGENT (build this)

Purpose: keep the funnel fresh automatically and feed the recycle / A-B testing engine.

- Scan the standing sources on a schedule (weekly is fine).
- Surface ONLY stories that fit the content rule: bad behavior by PBMs / TPAs / payers, denial
  and delay news, practice-economics pressure, access data, and legal wins. Discard anything
  that would require speaking negatively about dispensing, mail order, or WC pharmacy.
- For each kept item, draft a short "here is what this means for your practice" hook in the
  locked voice (hero = doctor + patient, villain = the middleman, our program = the counter),
  ready to drop in as a fresh opener for a recycle round or a new lead-magnet angle.
- Route drafts to Eric for a quick approve before they enter rotation.
- Feed the winners into the A-B test so the best-performing news angle takes over.

## ANCILLARY REVENUE RADAR (also build this into the agent)

Beyond marketing openers, watch for ancillary revenue opportunities or advancements the
practices (and we) should know about and could get involved in as an opportunity. Examples:
peptides for MSK / orthopedic and pain practices, regenerative and other cash-pay ancillary
lines, new compliant revenue models adjacent to WC pharmacy. Surface these to Eric as
intelligence (kind = "opportunity"), not as customer-facing copy, with what it is, why it
matters to our target practices, and how we might participate. Same approve gate.
